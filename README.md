## 🌐 About Us

The **IEEE Computer Society (CS) Student Branch Chapter** at the University of Sri Jayewardenepura is an official chapter of the **IEEE Student Branch – USJ**, established in **2018**. As part of the world's premier computing organization, we drive innovation, technical excellence, and professional growth among students in the fields of computer science, software engineering, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and beyond.

We operate in collaboration with the **IEEE Computer Society Sri Lanka Chapter (IEEE R10)** and are committed to nurturing future tech leaders while actively contributing to Sri Lanka's evolving technology ecosystem.

> *"Among all that arise, knowledge is the greatest."*  
> — Motto of the University of Sri Jayewardenepura

## 🚀 CS Chapter Projects

Our chapter drives a set of flagship projects that build technical depth, competitive skills, and community impact:

---

### ☁️ 1. Beauty of Cloud
> *Empowering students with modern cloud computing knowledge and skills.*

An initiative focused on cloud technologies — covering platforms, architectures, and real-world cloud deployment. Designed to bridge the gap between academia and industry-level cloud expertise.

---

### 🧠 2. AlgoAce
> *Sharpening algorithmic thinking and competitive programming skills.*

A structured program dedicated to Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA), preparing students for competitive programming contests, technical interviews, and problem-solving challenges at national and global levels.

---

### 🏆 3. J'pura Xtreme
> *A first-of-its-kind DSA hackathon at USJ.*

**J'puraXtreme Hackathon 1.0** was a landmark event at the University of Sri Jayewardenepura — an exhilarating DSA challenge that brought together teams of passionate programmers to compete, collaborate, and push the boundaries of algorithmic problem-solving.

---

### 🌍 4. IEEE Xtreme
> *Competing on the global stage.*

The CS Chapter actively supports student participation in **IEEE Xtreme** — a prestigious **24-hour global competitive programming challenge** organized by IEEE. Teams from USJ participate under the IEEE Student Branch, testing teamwork, endurance, and coding skills against students worldwide.

---

### 🏭 5. Industry 200
> *Connecting 200 students with industry insights.*

**Industry 200** is an outreach initiative aiming to connect 200 students with real-world industry professionals. Through sessions, talks, and networking opportunities, this project bridges the gap between university learning and the professional technology landscape.
